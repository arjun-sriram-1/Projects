CREDIT TENOR POLICY

Purpose:
Set maximum fuel trade credit tenor after reviewing PD, LGD, EAD, expected loss,
liquidity, leverage, market stress, and requested credit line.

Risk Grade B / High Risk:
Maximum 30 days unless fully cash-secured.

Risk Grade BB / Medium Risk:
Maximum 45 days, normally with standby letter of credit or bank guarantee.

Risk Grade BBB or better / Lower Risk:
Maximum 60-90 days if liquidity and debt service are adequate.

Any requested tenor above the recommended tenor requires Credit Committee review.
