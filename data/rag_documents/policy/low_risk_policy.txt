LOW RISK COUNTERPARTY POLICY

Applies when:
PD and expected loss are low, liquidity and debt service are strong, leverage is
acceptable, and market stress does not materially impair repayment capacity.

Credit line treatment:
Requested credit line may be approved when it is at or below the recommended
credit line and policy checks pass.

Maximum tenor:
60-90 days depending on financial strength and market regime.

Required security:
Optional for strong counterparties; guarantee or LC may still be required for
large requested lines or volatile fuel-market regimes.

Approval:
Relationship manager or credit manager according to exposure size.

Review frequency:
Semi-annual, or sooner if credit metrics deteriorate.
