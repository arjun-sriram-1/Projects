MEDIUM RISK COUNTERPARTY POLICY

Applies when:
The counterparty has moderate PD, acceptable but not strong liquidity, manageable
leverage, and no severe market stress override.

Credit line treatment:
Approve the requested line only if expected loss and policy score remain within
thresholds. Otherwise reduce the limit to the model-recommended line.

Maximum tenor:
45-60 days depending on liquidity and debt service.

Required security:
Corporate guarantee, standby LC, or bank guarantee when expected loss is elevated.

Approval:
Credit manager or committee depending on exposure size.

Review frequency:
Quarterly or after a material change in fuel, FX, or macro stress.
