SECURITY POLICY

Purpose:
Define acceptable protection when the requested fuel credit line creates
unacceptable expected loss, high LGD, weak liquidity, or adverse market exposure.

Acceptable security types:
1. Standby Letter of Credit
2. Bank Guarantee
3. Parent Company Guarantee
4. Cash Deposit
5. Secured collateral where enforceable

Preferred order:
Standby Letter of Credit
Bank Guarantee
Cash Deposit
Parent Guarantee
Secured collateral

Security should be required when:
Recommended limit is below requested limit, LGD is elevated, expected loss is
above policy appetite, tenor is longer than recommended, or commodity/FX stress
materially affects the counterparty.
