HIGH RISK COUNTERPARTY POLICY

Applies when:
PD is elevated, expected loss is high relative to requested line, liquidity is weak,
or market stress materially affects the counterparty.

Credit line treatment:
Reduce requested credit line to the model-recommended limit.

Maximum tenor:
30 days.

Required security:
Standby Letter of Credit, bank guarantee, cash deposit, or other strong collateral.

Approval:
Credit Committee / senior credit approver.

Review frequency:
Monthly or after major market stress events.
